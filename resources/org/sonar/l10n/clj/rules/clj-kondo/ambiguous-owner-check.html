Why is this an issue?

A nil owner means two different things — untenanted and shared by design, or a lookup that failed — and a two-valued test gives both the same branch.

This is WEB-184: the cross-tenant guard answered a three-valued question with two values, and both answers allowed.

How can I fix it?

Make the third state representable and deny on it:

[:or [:= ::untenanted] :uuid [:= ::unresolved]]
