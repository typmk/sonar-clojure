Why is this an issue?

A quantified group whose body is also quantified — (a+)+, ([a-z]*)* — makes the engine try every way of splitting the input. A short crafted string costs seconds of CPU.

Clojure regex literals compile to java.util.regex.Pattern, so the exposure is exactly Java's.

How can I fix it?

Flatten the nesting, or bound the repetition:

#"[a-z]+"        ; not #"([a-z]+)+"
#"[a-z]{1,64}"
