Why is this an issue?

Two or more parameters, a docstring of 25 words or fewer, and none of the parameters named in it. A reader at the call site has the arglist, not the body; a docstring that does not say what each argument must be leaves them to read the implementation.

How can I fix it?

Name each parameter and what it must be. Long docstrings that explain the design are exempt by the length guard: measured on defnet without it, 272 hits, every one a design docstring.
